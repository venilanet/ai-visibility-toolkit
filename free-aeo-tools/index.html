7 Free (and Cheap) AI Visibility Tools to Track Competitor Citations in 2026

Here's the problem: you want to know whether ChatGPT recommends you or your competitors — but every AI visibility tool seems priced for agencies. If you've ever thought "a tiny subscription would be much preferable to agency-level pricing," this guide is for you.

Below: one genuinely free method, one free one-off grader, and five affordable trackers — compared with real 2026 prices, so you can see exactly what "cheap" buys you.

Why Everyone's Asking for a Free AEO Tool

AI search changed the game. Buyers now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews for recommendations instead of scrolling Google results — and the brands cited in those answers win the click. Naturally, everyone wants to track their share of AI citations.

The frustration is price. Enterprise platforms charge hundreds per month because every tracked prompt costs real API money across multiple models, daily. But most solo founders and small teams don't need 10,000 prompts tracked — they need to answer one question: when someone asks AI for a recommendation in my category, does it name me or my competitors?

That question has cheaper answers than the enterprise sales pages suggest. Let's go through them from free upward.

What to Look for (Before You Compare Prices)

Cheap is only good if the tool answers your actual question. Check these three things:

  1. Competitor citation tracking, not just your own brand. The whole point is the gap: who gets recommended when you're not. If a tool only tracks your brand mentions, it can't show you that gap.
  2. Mention vs. recommendation. Being named in passing while the AI recommends three competitors is not a win. Good tools distinguish the two.
  3. Full prompt text and dates stored. You need to see which prompts triggered citations and when, or the data isn't actionable.

With that lens, here are the seven options, ordered by price.

1. Manual prompt panels — free forever

Cost: $0. The most honest "free tool" on this list is a method, not software.

Write 10–20 variations of the buying-intent questions your customers actually ask (e.g., "best CRM for a 5-person startup," "what's a good alternative to [competitor]?"). Run each one in ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. Record which brands get cited versus merely mentioned in every answer, in a spreadsheet, with dates.

It takes 15–30 minutes per check and doesn't scale — but it directly answers the competitor-gap question, and it's how you should verify any paid tool before subscribing (more on that below). Do this weekly for a month before spending a dollar.

Choose this if: your budget is zero and you track fewer than ~20 prompts. Every paid tool below is just automation of this workflow.

2. HubSpot AI Search Grader — free one-off check

HubSpot offers a free AI search grader: enter your brand and get a one-time snapshot of your AI visibility. It's a single scan, not ongoing tracking — useful as a baseline ("where do I stand today?") before you decide whether ongoing tracking is worth paying for.

Choose this if: you want a free starting measurement with zero commitment.

How to Verify a Tracker's Numbers Before You Pay

Never subscribe on marketing claims alone. Here's the 15-minute verification:

  1. Pick 10 prompts your buyers actually ask.
  2. Run them manually in ChatGPT and Perplexity; note which brands are cited vs. mentioned.
  3. Start the tool's free trial and run the same 10 prompts.
  4. Compare. If the tool's data roughly matches what you saw by hand, its methodology is sound.
  5. Red flag: a tool that reports "mentions" but can't show you whether the AI recommended anyone. That's the metric that matters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a free AI visibility tool?

For ongoing automated tracking, truly free options are rare because every AI query costs the vendor money. Your free options: the manual prompt-panel method (free forever, 15–30 min per check) and HubSpot's free one-off AI Search Grader for a baseline snapshot.

What is the cheapest AI visibility tracker?

Rankscale (~€20/month) and Otterly.ai (~$29/month) are the cheapest dedicated trackers reported in 2026. Both are EU-based with simpler feature sets than mid-tier tools.

Can I track competitor citations without paying?

Yes — run 10–20 buying-intent prompt variations manually across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini, and log which brands get cited vs. mentioned. It's manual, but it directly reveals the competitor gap.

Why are AI visibility tools so expensive?

Each tracked prompt costs real API money across multiple AI models, every day. Enterprise tools run thousands of prompts and charge accordingly. Budget tools keep prices down by limiting prompt counts and engine coverage.

How do I verify a tracker's numbers before paying?

Run 10 prompts manually, then compare against the tool's trial results. If they roughly match, the methodology is sound. Avoid tools that report mentions without distinguishing recommendations.
